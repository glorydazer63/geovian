## Some Tips For You
As an "over user" of X, I've found ways to be more productive about using the platform more than most. Here are a few of my tips.

### Use a VPN
**Use a reputable VPN** - this is a must when using X as recent sweeping events related to infringements on freedom of speech in countries like the UK, France and Brazil have come to pass. The two VPN's I use are Mullvad and Private Internet Access (PIA). A third that I would have no hesitation recommending is SurfShark.

### Use the Web Browser
**Web Browser rather than App** - I am rather suspicious of large scale mobile apps like X, Facebook, Reddit, Instagram etc due to privacy concerns. Instead I corral my X interactions using the Brave browser, which immediately puts a halt on X's advertising which they overload through the app. I'm not a fan of X's notifications constantly flowing through either, though I can control this via my phones notification settings. Using a web browser means I can just focus on the content only.

### Obfuscation
**Never use your real name** - obfuscation is the key when playing around on X. Avoid using real names, country of origin, place or locations that are local to you, don't use a photo of you (if you do use a real old one or an unrelated graphic) or of your family, or your car and home. Hopefully the reasons why will be obvious to you, as in.. identity theft.

### Use Block Option
**Use the block option constantly** - there are many idiots on X. Know how to identify them and block them like the plague. 

### Use Mute Option
**Use the Mute Word option to filter/block content** - X throws all manner of content your way thanks to their algorithms. Under your Profile, go to:

- Settings and Privacy
- go to Privacy and Safety
- then Mute and Block
- then Muted Words

- Enter in a term (like Kamala)
- then click **SAVE** button at the bottom of the screen
- Enter a new term and repeat.

### Use Fedica App
**Use the Fedica app** - I was recommended this app to provide some value added tools to my X account, not provided by X itself. Fedica does two useful things for me:

  - provides me useful weekly statistics on who has unhallowed me on X. More out of curiosity than exactful revenge lol. 
  - a means of writing posts, though any draft content beyond 300 characters (the limit for non blue checked accounts) is broken out into a continuous thread. A blue check account will show up as post.
