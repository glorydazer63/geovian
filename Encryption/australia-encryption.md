## Australian Government - 'pie in the sky' on Encryption

<img src="assets/austgovt.jpg" />

_Knowing what I know about Government, if I had my way I'd sack all Parliaments, all Governments and all public servants and we can start again from scratch.

**Written By:** Geovian
**Date:** June 20, 2021

They just cannot be trusted to do the right thing on behalf of the people that they're supposed to be serving. This is definitely a case of cart before horse.

History will often show that the wrong sort of people get into power and stay there. These are not the sort of people you want to manage populations and society. Does Robert Mugabe and Zimbabwe ring a bell anyone? How about Justin Trudeau and Canada? I can see in future, that people will rise up and they will hold Governments, councils and local authorities to account.

Here in Australia, senior politicians don't really get to hang around too long, because they often get stabbed in the back by their own colleagues and next thing you know we find that a political party has a new leader, hence a new prime minister.

This is tantamount to treachery. In the last decade, we've seen Kevin Rudd, Julia Gillard, Tony Abbott and Malcolm Turnbull all depart the Prime Ministership by way of the sword. In Gillard's' case, the sword was rendered by the voting public.

Yet, the Australian Government is embroiled in more controversy. Scott (Sco Mo) Morrison is now the Prime Minister. The Home Affairs Minister Peter Dutton has more enemies than friends, and ludicrous legislation is popping out of the woodwork like termites looking for their next block of wood.

One of those legislative pieces of art is the "Telecommunications and Other Legislation Amendment (Assistance and Access) Bill 2018". After much gnashing of teeth and shouting from the rooftops, the Australian Government moved to placate the nation by ensuring that no back door decryption would be forced upon Internet Providers and other existing tools of trade.

With Google, Facebook and other Tech Giants putting tons of resources into encryption, it will be a hard sell for Government to plead their case. Though they say they are only trying to catch out the bad guys, everybody gets caught up in their Orwellian dragnet. If they can get away with it, they will.

As ZDNet Australian Editor Chris Duckett once pointed out awhile back: "The core issue is that all these schemes boil down to putting a genie back in the bottle, and to mix analogies, not only has the encrypted messaging horse bolted, but it is three paddocks over and never coming back to the stable."

China has set the bar for using technology to manage its population in a totalitarian way. The Australian Government can only dream that they too can have an overreaching system to manage its population. The problem is, they will never get there because of three things:

  - technology is ever-changing. Cyber security, hardened encryption and possibly blockchain.
  - the Australian people will not allow this to happen, over their dead bodies.
  - any Australian Government attempting to do this will be booted out of office.

There endth the sermon.
