# Nostr - the alternative ecosystem
I have been using the **Nostr** framework and protocol for a few months now. It's designed to be a decentralized ecosystem akin to Facebook and Twitter/X, with a focus on censorship resistance and privacy.

Because of its protocol, no centralised entity or corporation can impact your footprint, because they don't (or can't) own anything.

The Nostr development community is very active. There are a load of apps that are popular ranging from social media clients (think Facebook, X, Instagram, Tiktok, Blue Sky), privacy chat tools, long form and short form editors, tasks and to-do lists, calendars, email, VPN, storage repositories, online web publishing, crypto wallets and much more. 

Here, we briefly touch on a few standouts within each category.

## Compulsory

Before installing any app, a Nostr signing app like Amber is required so it can build your prequisites I fo such as public and private keys as well as default relays you would use to get your content out onto the Nostr ecosystem.

## Social Media Clients
### Amethyst
A long standing client in Nostr is Amethyst. It has the traditional look and feel of a social media platform like X. 

Available on [Zapstore](https://zapstore.dev/apps/naddr1qqdxxmmd9emxjar0wfcxzmtsd3hkucfwv9kk2arg09ehgqgcwaehxw309aex2mrp0yh85ctswd6x7un99ejx2aszyz4fq3ej2cpa4n20s9pqjdt8ju6kdh3mrcs2392hku5v80jvd2zykqcyqqq8uzczqxd3s)

### Ditto
This client has an interesting quirky interface. It's quite a busy layout and the posts layout does not include all previous posts displayed. You have to click into the post itself to view them. I guess there's a reason for that. Economies visual real estate. 

Available on [Zapstore](https://zapstore.dev/apps/naddr1qqxhqatz9ejxjar5duhxzursqyv8wumn8ghj7un9d3shjtn6v9c8xar0wfjjuer9wcpzq7q6z5ns2hm5c8msyv83qwzxpxe52j8c4d4q5m92wsp9sflelkh9qvzqqqr7pvxtj7r6)

### Nostria
I quite like this client. The layout is excellent though it seems there are some glitches as it crashes on occasion. The dual black and orange colour layout is a bit stark. Probably needs a bit of refining.

Available on [Zapstore](https://zapstore.dev/apps/naddr1qq9kzurs9ehx7um5wf5kzqgcwaehxw309aex2mrp0yh85ctswd6x7un99ejx2aszyrgm6venxueae3q37rhgjwechpfzlsx7yfll73vanyzyemv7v4vpkqcyqqq8uzc0jeyy9)
