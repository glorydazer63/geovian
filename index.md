This site features a collection of tech related articles relating to software, hardware, innovations plus industry trends. We've been off/online since 2020, but we're looking to pick up the pace in 2026 and beyond.

- advocating for the use of small factor devices for business use.
- reporting on new tech initiatives as it relates to small business.

Our focus is mainly on Australian and New Zealand business communities.
